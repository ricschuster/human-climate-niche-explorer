# Session Handoff: 2026-11-13 (preprocessing and site)

## Summary

Built the preprocessing pipeline with a validation script, then the
Observable Framework site with every MVP view. The pipeline reproduces the
paper (107 of 107 checks pass) and the site builds from the committed
processed data. The work is in two open PRs: #2 (preprocessing) and #3 (site),
with #3 stacked on #2. Next: merge them, then deploy to GitHub Pages.

## Completed

- PR #2 `feat/preprocessing` (CI green):
  - `scripts/build_global.py`, `build_niche_curves.py`, `build_countries.py`
    and `build_map_layers.py` write `data/processed/`.
  - `scripts/validate.py` checks the outputs against the paper's text and
    captions, the 65 points of Fig. 3a and the Fig. 5 spreadsheet country by
    country.
  - `scripts/build_all.sh` runs all of it; a rebuild gives byte-identical
    outputs.
  - The repo gains `ruff.toml` and a CI `python-lint` job.
- ADR 0004 refined: exposed land area now uses exact cell-polygon coverage on
  the WGS84 ellipsoid, which reproduces every country's land fraction
  (including small islands).
- `docs/design/01_data_notes.md` records the differences from the paper:
  - The authors divide the 11.1 billion percentages by the 9.5 billion total,
    which is where the paper's 17.5 % per C comes from.
  - Two percentages in the paper's text are rounded off.
  - The paper converts percent to people with a nominal 9.5 billion.
  - Global and country hot exposure are counted differently, so they don't
    add up.
- PR #3 `feat/site`, stacked on #2:
  - The site: `src/index.md`, `src/methods.md` and components for the slider,
    map, charts and formatting.
  - `docs/design/02_site.md` and a CI `site-build` job.
  - Map rasters are now split into west and east halves to fit mobile GPU
    texture limits.
