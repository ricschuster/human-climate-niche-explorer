## Current state

What was verified this session, with throwaway scripts that were not
committed (rebuild them properly in `scripts/`):

- `displacement_data.mat` (MATLAB v5, reads with `scipy.io.loadmat`; load
  only the variables you need with `variable_names=`, about 3 s and 360 MB per
  scenario). 2,287,025 land cells at 1/12 degree; `x`, `y` are cell centres.
  Raster index: `col = round((x + 180) * 12 - 0.5)`,
  `row = round((90 - y) * 12 - 0.5)` on a 4320 x 2160 grid.
- Variables: `MAT_Sk_yy` / `MAP_Sk_yy` (cells x models; k = SSP 1, 2, 3, 5;
  yy = 30, 50, 70, 90 for 2021-2040 ... 2081-2100), `POP_Sk_yy` (counts),
  `MAT0020` (5 datasets, 2000-2020), `MAT6090` (3 datasets, 1960-1990),
  `popcount1980`, `popcount2010`.
- Warming level to MAT variable (paper Methods): 1.5 = `MAT_S1_30`,
  1.8 = `MAT_S1_90`, 2.0 = `MAT_S2_50`, 2.1 = `MAT_S3_50`, 2.4 = `MAT_S5_50`,
  2.7 = `MAT_S2_90`, 3.6 = `MAT_S3_90`, 4.4 = `MAT_S5_90`. Fixed populations:
  6.9 bn = `popcount2010`, 9.5 bn = `POP_S2_70`, 11.1 bn = `POP_S3_70`.
- Hot exposure uses MAT strictly greater than 29 (the authors' code), although
  the paper writes "29 degrees C or more"; it makes no practical difference.
- Global per-model exposure (mean and sd across models) reproduces Fig. 2 and
  3: SSP2 2090 gives 23.2 plus or minus 9.1 percent (paper 23 plus or minus
  9); 2010 gives 0.9 percent.
- Country method (ADR 0004): ensemble-mean MAT, then threshold, cell centre in
  polygon, World Borders 0.3 reproduces Fig. 5 exactly (1,997 M and 419 M);
  Natural Earth gives 1,994 M and 419 M. Land fraction divides by polygon area.
- Cell area used: `(6371.0088 * deg2rad(1/12))**2 * cos(deg2rad(y))` km2;
  exposed land area matches the paper to within 0.3 percent.
- Map test: a 4320 x 2160 RGBA PNG of the 2.7 degree mask is 83 KB, the
  1.5 degree mask 50 KB, a 9-class population density PNG 314 KB.
- Tooling: system Python 3.14 has no scipy or pandas; use
  `uv run --with scipy --with numpy ...` (add `geopandas pyogrio openpyxl
  pillow` as needed). Node 24 is available for Observable Framework.
- Not readable outside MATLAB: `CMIP6_WBT28` in `extremetemp.mat` (MATLAB
  table object). Out of scope per the brief.
